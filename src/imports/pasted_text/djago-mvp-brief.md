1. Graphic Design & Branding
2. Civil Engineering
3. Interior Design

### BRAND IDENTITY

Company name:
**DJAGO Design & Build**

Primary brand:
**DJAGO**

Brand descriptor:
**DESIGN • ENGINEERING • INTERIORS**

Primary slogan:
**Your Vision • Our Expertise**

Use the existing DJAGO logo concept as the central visual identity. The logo consists of a strong geometric **D/J monogram**, architectural/construction-inspired linework, the **DJAGO** wordmark, and a premium gold-and-black aesthetic.

The website must feel:

* Premium

# DESIGN SYSTEM

### Primary colours

Use this colour palette:

* Gold: **#D4AF37**
* Black: **#111111**
* Charcoal: **#333333**
* Gray: **#9CA3AF**
* Off-white: **#F5F5F5**
* White: **#FFFFFF**

Use black/charcoal as the dominant colour, gold as an accent, and white/off-white for clean content sections.

Gold should be used carefully for:

* Buttons
* Icons
* Section accents
* Hover states
* Borders
* Important headings
* Architectural line details

Do not overuse gold.

### Typography

Use:

* **Montserrat** for headings, navigation and major titles
* **Lato** for body text and supporting information

Use strong typography, generous spacing, clean grids and precise alignment.
